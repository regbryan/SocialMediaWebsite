# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Founders, marketing leads, and personal-brand operators evaluating a social media content agency. They land here from referrals, IG/LinkedIn, or direct outreach and need to decide in under a minute whether SocialPulse Media can produce content that looks more expensive than what they have today. The audience spans local SMBs (restaurants, retail, services in LA / Inland Empire), mid-market brands and franchises with retainer budgets, and personal brands (real estate, coaches, creators). The shared job to be done: see proof, gauge fit, and book a call without friction.

## Product Purpose

A marketing site whose primary KPI is qualified booked calls. It must:
1. Prove production quality through actual client work, not adjectives.
2. Communicate that the agency is premium without crossing into corporate stiffness or influencer hype.
3. Route visitors to one of three actions: book a call, view portfolio, or log in to the client dashboard.

The site IS the product demo. If the design doesn't look like something a creator would pay for, the pitch fails.

## Brand Personality

Premium, cinematic, confident. Reads like a creative agency reel, not a SaaS pitch. Voice is plain-spoken and direct — "we make your content look like this" — not jargon-heavy or aspirational fluff. Visuals carry the personality; copy stays tight and earns attention rather than demanding it.

## Anti-references

- **Influencer / clout aesthetic.** No chrome text, no neon, no TikTok-bait motion, no "🔥💯" energy. We sell to people who hire creators, not creators themselves.
- **Corporate B2B / enterprise.** No stock handshake photography, no navy + gray, no dense paragraph copy, no "leverage / synergize" language, no trust-badge graveyard.
- Generic Wix/Squarespace agency template look (three-up icon grid, pastel gradients, stock hero).
- SaaS landing-page template (hero metric block, repeating feature columns, big purple CTA-as-personality).

## Design Principles

1. **Show the work, not the story about the work.** Every section should answer "what does their content actually look like?" before it answers anything else. Portfolio, reels, and client logos do more selling than headlines.
2. **Premium through restraint, not decoration.** Silver frames, dark canvas, real motion. We earn the "premium" read by being quiet where SaaS templates are loud, and dense where they are sparse.
3. **One accent color, used like a signature.** Purple is the brand mark, not the wallpaper. Used to point, not to fill.
4. **Motion is choreography, not garnish.** Marquees, hover lifts, video autoplay — each motion exists to make the work feel alive. No bounce, no parallax tricks, no scroll-jacking.
5. **Conversion lives at the bottom of every screen.** A visitor should never be more than one scroll from a "book a call" affordance, but the affordance should feel inevitable, not aggressive.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA minimum on text contrast against the `#07070e` canvas. Body text never lighter than `#bfbfcc`; muted text never used for actionable information.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: marquees, video autoplay, fade-up entrances must pause or disable.
- All portfolio media must have descriptive `alt` text — these are decorative for sighted users but informational for the screen-reader audience evaluating us.
- Keyboard: lightbox, login, and CTA must be focus-visible and operable without a pointer.
